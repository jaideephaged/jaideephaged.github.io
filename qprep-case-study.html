Why I Made This

During exam season, I observed that students spend more time searching for previous year question papers than actually revising.

They often:

  • Visit multiple websites
  • Download scattered PDFs
  • Manually check repeated questions
  • Waste time identifying important patterns

This inspired me to design a centralized solution that saves time and reduces exam stress.

The Problem

Students lack a single organized platform to:

  • Access all previous year question papers
  • Identify frequently repeated questions
  • Spot unique or tricky patterns
  • Filter content efficiently

Core Issues

  • Scattered resources
  • Manual analysis required
  • No repetition tracking
  • Time wasted before exams

Design Direction

  • Centralized repository
  • Smart filtering system
  • Clear tagging for repeated questions
  • Minimal and distraction-free dashboard

Goal: Reduce cognitive load during high-pressure exam time.

USER FLOW

The flow was intentionally kept flat to minimize navigation depth and reduce friction.

Information Architecture

Home ├── Subjects │ ├── Year-wise Papers │ ├── Repeated Questions │ └── Unique Questions ├── Search └── Saved Questions

Wireframes

Key Features

  • Centralized question paper database
  • Smart repetition filter
  • Unique question identification
  • Subject-wise & year-wise categorization
  • Quick search functionality

Constraints

  • 2-week timeline
  • Solo designer
  • No backend data automation
  • Focused on UI & structure

What I Learned

  • Information hierarchy directly impacts efficiency
  • Filtering reduces cognitive load
  • Simplicity improves usability under stress
  • Utility apps must prioritize clarity over aesthetics

Interview Summary

QPrep is a web-based exam preparation platform that helps students save time by organizing and filtering previous year question papers intelligently, reducing manual effort during revision.
