Why I Made This

Many users depend on physical mouse devices to operate their laptops. But situations like:

• Forgetting the mouse
• Battery dying
• Misplacing it
• Trackpad discomfort

Inspired me to design a simple backup solution using a device everyone already has — their smartphone.

The Problem

Users need a quick and reliable way to control their computer when a physical mouse is unavailable.

Core Issues:

  • No quick backup option
  • Existing apps feel complicated
  • Poor connection feedback
  • Cluttered interfaces

USER FLOW

The flow was intentionally designed to minimize steps and reduce friction during connection.

DESIGN DECISIONS

Full-Screen Touch Area

Maximizes cursor control precision.

Clear Status Indicator

Prevents confusion about connection state.

Minimal Interface

Avoids unnecessary complexity.

What I Learned

  • Utility apps demand clarity over aesthetics
  • Status feedback is critical in connected systems
  • Simplicity improves user confidence
  • Interaction space must be prioritized
