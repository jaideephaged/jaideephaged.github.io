Why I Worked on This

A local money lender was maintaining loan records using physical notebooks, Excel sheets, and manual calculations.

This caused confusion in tracking repayments, manual interest miscalculations, difficulty identifying overdue payments, and no centralized system.

The goal was to create a structured digital system that automates calculations and simplifies tracking.

The Problem

Small local lenders lack an organized and automated system to manage loans.

Core Issues

  • Manual record keeping
  • Frequent calculation errors
  • No real-time due tracking
  • No risk visibility
  • Difficult borrower history access

The lender needed a system that reduces manual effort and prevents financial mistakes.

Research & Validation

I directly spoke with the lender to understand how he tracks loans, where mistakes happen, how interest is calculated, and what information he checks daily.

  • Recalculating interest repeatedly
  • Forgetting due dates
  • Not knowing total pending amount instantly

Design Direction

  • Automation over manual entry
  • Simple loan creation flow
  • Smart calculation engine
  • Clear financial visibility
  • Risk-based visual indicators

Goal: Make loan tracking effortless and error-free.

Key Features

Dual Loan Types

  • Smart Interest Plan (Bank-style calculation)
  • Fixed Return Plan (Fixed periodic return model)

Smart Loan Creation

  • Auto total payable calculation
  • Next due date generation
  • EMI breakdown
  • Pending balance tracking

Automation Engine

  • Automatic interest calculation
  • Late fee auto-calculation
  • Overdue alerts
  • Dynamic payment updates

Risk & Analytics Dashboard

  • Color-coded risk levels
  • Total active loan summary
  • Overdue loan count
  • Revenue overview
  • Payment trend visualization

User Flow

The flow was designed to minimize steps while ensuring complete financial accuracy.

Information Architecture

Dashboard ├── Active Loans ├── Overdue Loans ├── Borrowers ├── Analytics └── Settings Loan Detail ├── Payment History ├── Upcoming Due ├── Penalties └── Loan Summary
